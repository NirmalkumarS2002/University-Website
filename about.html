Our Story

Founded On A Simple Idea

University of Stackly began with one belief, that quality education should be shaped around real students, not the other way around. What started as a single faculty building in Melbourne has grown into a full campus serving thousands of learners from over 60 countries.

Today, we combine research-driven teaching with hands-on industry exposure, so every graduate leaves with both knowledge and the confidence to use it.
